The flip side of that: auto-renewal is the default, and RackNerd's terms require a cancellation notice **5 days before the renewal date**, submitted through the client area. ## Which plan actually fits your site

The tiers differ in three ways: storage, bandwidth, and how many websites you can host. But here's the detail most comparison articles miss: **the CloudLinux resource limits are identical on every plan** — 100% of one CPU core, 1 GB of physical memory, and 80 MB/s I/O per account. Upgrading does not buy you more CPU or RAM. It buys more disk, more bandwidth, and more domains.

That changes the decision math completely:

- **30 GB / $10.49/yr** — 3 sites total, 3 TB of monthly bandwidth. A typical WordPress install with a theme and ~20 plugins runs 500 MB to 1 GB, and most content sites stay under 10 GB of disk even after years of images. For one blog or a small business site, this plan is all you need.
- **85 GB / $24.99/yr** — 11 sites total and 10 TB of bandwidth. This is the freelancer or multi-site tier.
- **200 GB / $59.00/yr** — unlimited domains, 30 TB of bandwidth. Only worth it if you're genuinely hosting many sites or running storage-heavy media.

A single high-traffic site will hit the same CloudLinux ceiling regardless of tier, so don't upsize expecting more headroom for one busy site. Upsize for the domain count or storage you actually need. And if what you really want is more raw resources, that's a VPS conversation, not a hosting-tier conversation (more on that below).
